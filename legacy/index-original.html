These are the rules for FSRP — Discord, in-session, applications, tickets, everywhere. Everyone's expected to know them, not just skim them.

Ask staff through support →
01

Community Conduct

  • Respect members, staff, departments, and partners.
  • No harassment, discrimination, threats, trolling, or targeted disruption.
  • No impersonating staff, departments, or whitelisted ranks.
  • No inappropriate usernames, avatars, messages, or filter bypassing.
02

Roleplay Standards

  • No RDM, VDM, mass RDM, mass VDM, or random attacks.
  • No fail roleplay, NITRP, cop baiting, cuff rushing, or tool abuse.
  • Follow Fear Roleplay and New Life Rule expectations.
  • Do not enter or interfere with scenes without a valid roleplay reason.
03

Vehicles & Driving

  • Use approved vehicles, liveries, and emergency equipment.
  • No GTA driving, unrealistic speeds, vehicle ramming, or vehicle abuse.
  • Do not tow, block, or damage staff vehicles during moderation scenes.
  • Follow traffic laws unless a valid roleplay situation requires otherwise.
04

Uniforms & Equipment

  • Use approved uniforms, callsigns, liveries, weapons, and tools.
  • No banned vehicles, weapons, avatars, or glitch accessories.
  • Department equipment must only be used for official duties.
  • Custom assets must follow department and server standards.
05

Priority & Scenes

  • Respect the priority timer and current priority status.
  • Do not begin major scenes during cooldown or restricted status.
  • Sensitive roleplays require staff approval before they begin.
  • Follow safe-zone restrictions and active scene boundaries.
06

Staff Interactions

  • Do not interfere with, evade, or leave an active moderation scene.
  • Answer staff honestly and provide evidence when requested.
  • No lying to staff, staff evasion, or early rejoining after a kick.
  • Use reports and appeals instead of arguing inside the session.
07

Platform Safety

  • Follow Roblox and Discord rules at all times.
  • No exploiting, bug abuse, account compromise, or malicious links.
  • Never share private information or pressure others to share it.
  • Report serious safety concerns directly to leadership.
08

Session Compliance

  • Join only after an official session announcement.
  • Follow SSU, SSD, restart, queue, and server-full instructions.
  • Leave promptly during shutdown unless staff instruct otherwise.
  • Repeated disruption may restrict access to future sessions.

ENFORCEMENT FRAMEWORK

CONSISTENT. DOCUMENTED. FAIR.

LEVEL 01

Verbal Warning

A heads-up for a small, first-time issue. Fix it and move on.

Examples: incorrect livery, uniform issue, unrealistic avatar, minor callsign issue.
LEVEL 02

Formal Warning

An actual mark on your record — for a clear violation, or doing the small stuff more than once.

Examples: RDM, VDM, fail roleplay, NLR, cop baiting, tool abuse.
LEVEL 03

Session Kick

You're out of the session immediately. Used when someone's actively ruining it for everyone else.

Examples: mass RDM/VDM, staff evasion, lying to staff, three warnings.
LEVEL 04

Community Ban

Reserved for the serious stuff — repeated offenses, anything unsafe, or breaking Roblox/Discord's own rules.

Examples: LTAP, raiding, exploiting, severe trolling, platform violations.
STAFF DISCRETION

Staff weigh intent, history, and how serious it actually was — so the same rule can land differently depending on the situation. Anything that's a safety issue or breaks Roblox/Discord's rules can skip straight to a harsher response.

Staff Code & Ethics

Staff must remain respectful, neutral, and professional. Staff may not use permissions for personal advantage, retaliate against members, reveal confidential records, falsify evidence, or moderate a situation where a serious conflict of interest exists.

Moderation actions should be supported by logs, screenshots, recordings, witness statements, or direct staff observation whenever practical.

Department Standards

Department members must follow their chain of command, maintain approved uniforms and callsigns, complete required training, remain reasonably active, and represent FSRP professionally.

Promotions are earned through performance, maturity, activity, leadership, training completion, and department need—not friendship or payment.

Applications & Transfers

Applications must be truthful and completed by the applicant. Plagiarism, alternate-account evasion, falsified experience, or pressure on reviewers may result in denial and an application cooldown.

Transfer or fast-pass requests do not guarantee a rank. Leadership may require verification, interviews, training, or probation.

Appeals & Reports

Appeals must identify the action being appealed, explain why review is justified, and include available evidence. Harassment, spam, dishonesty, or repeated appeals without new information may close the case.

Reports are confidential when possible, but information may be shared with authorized reviewers when needed to investigate fairly.

Marketplace & Digital Benefits

Only purchases made through official FSRP channels are recognized. Digital roles or benefits have no cash value and may be changed, suspended, or removed when required for security, platform compliance, fraud prevention, or serious rule violations.

Purchase concerns must be handled through an official purchase-support ticket. Never send payment or account information to an individual claiming to represent FSRP.

TERMS OF SERVICE

Use of FSRP Services

By joining or using Florida State Roleplay services, you agree to follow this website, the official Discord rules, staff instructions, and the rules of Roblox, Discord, and ER:LC.

  • You must meet the minimum age and account requirements of the platforms you use.
  • You are responsible for your account, messages, applications, purchases, and in-game actions.
  • You may not exploit, attack, disrupt, copy, impersonate, or misuse FSRP systems or branding.
  • Access may be limited or removed for violations, safety concerns, fraud, or platform compliance.
  • FSRP features, departments, benefits, and services may change during development.
PRIVACY NOTICE

Information We Handle

FSRP may process information members provide or generate through normal community use.

  • Discord user ID, username, roles, join information, and public profile details.
  • Applications, tickets, reports, appeals, moderation records, and bot logs.
  • Purchase-support records when a member requests assistance.
  • Data is used for operations, safety, moderation, applications, support, and recordkeeping.
  • Access should be limited to authorized staff with a legitimate community purpose.
PLATFORM NOTICE

Independent Community

Florida State Roleplay is an independent fan-operated roleplay community and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Roblox Corporation, Police Roleplay Community, or Emergency Response: Liberty County.

  • Roblox and Discord accounts remain governed by their respective platforms.
  • FSRP cannot restore accounts, Robux, platform purchases, or external services.
  • Never share passwords, authentication codes, cookies, or private account credentials.
  • Official staff will not ask members for passwords or login codes.
Service Availability and Changes

Sessions, bots, forms, links, departments, marketplace items, and other services may be changed, paused, replaced, or discontinued. FSRP does not guarantee uninterrupted access.

Digital Purchases and Refund Requests

Digital benefits are delivered according to the offer shown at purchase time. Refund or delivery concerns must be reviewed through official purchase support. Fraudulent disputes, impersonation, or chargeback abuse may result in restrictions.

Suspension and Termination

FSRP may suspend or terminate access for rule violations, platform violations, security threats, evasion, fraud, harassment, or serious disruption. Eligible moderation actions may be appealed through the official process.

Policy Updates

Rules and policies may be updated as the community grows. Important changes should be announced through official FSRP channels. Continued use after an update means members are expected to follow the updated standards.
